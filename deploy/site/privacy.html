Privacy Policy

Orsuun: War of Banners · last updated 24 September 2026

What we collect

  • A random device token the app creates on first launch, used as your guest account. It is not your phone's advertising ID or hardware ID.
  • Your game progress: items, currencies, levels, stages, and a log of game actions (forging, trades, rewards) kept to keep the game fair and to resolve support requests.
  • Your IP address when an account is created, used only to limit how many new accounts one network can create per day (abuse protection).
  • Error reports if the app hits a bug: the error text, the app version and your platform. They contain no personal data.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, no ad tracking, no selling or sharing of data with third parties.
  • No contacts, photos, location, microphone or camera access.

Where it is stored

On our game server hosted by Hetzner in the European Union (Helsinki, Finland).

Deleting your data

Open the in-game MENU and tap DELETE ACCOUNT. This permanently deletes your account, items, game log and error reports from our server. It cannot be undone.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.
