Orsuun: War of Banners

An idle side-scrolling battler of the steppe for iPhone and Android.

  • Your hero hunts on their own, even while you are away.
  • Forge your gear up to +9, but from +4 a failed forge can break it.
  • Swear to one of three Banners, join a guild, and fight for the fortresses.

Orsuun is in a closed playtest. It is not in the App Store or Google Play yet.
